# PrimeLux Events - Core Features

PrimeLux Events is a premium event rental platform designed to provide a seamless, high-end experience for both clients and administrators.

## 1. Product Catalog & Discovery
- **Visual Catalog**: High-resolution image galleries for all rental items.
- **Advanced Filtering**: Filter by category (Seating, Tables, Decor, etc.) to find the perfect pieces.
- **Product Details**: Detailed specifications, pricing, and availability for every item.

## 2. Online Quote & Booking System
- **Self-Service Cart**: Add items to a personalized rental list.
- **Instant Quotes**: Real-time pricing calculations based on selected items and quantities.
- **Availability Checking**: Automatically verifies inventory for your selected event dates.
- **Secure Reservation**: Book and pay deposits online via Stripe integration.

## 3. Customer Experience
- **Customer Accounts**: Manage bookings, view past orders, and save favorites.
- **Appointment Scheduling**: Book showroom viewings directly through the site.
- **Consultation Leads**: Submit inquiries for custom events and design services.

## 4. Inspiration & Portfolio
- **Portfolio Gallery**: Browse past events to see our collection in action.
- **The Edit (Blog)**: Stay updated with the latest trends and expert advice in luxury event styling.
- **Aesthetic Excellence**: A modern, responsive design with smooth animations and a premium feel.

## 5. Logistics & Support
- **Delivery Coordination**: Automated tracking and scheduling for deliveries.
- **Clear Policies**: Transparent information on rental periods, damage waivers, and cancellations.
